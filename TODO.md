# Project Roadmap & New Editions TODO 🚀

This document outlines all official UNO editions, card counts, rules, mechanics, and features planned for the game.

---

## 🎴 Official UNO Editions & Deck Sizes to Support

### 1. 💥 UNO Show 'Em No Mercy (168 Cards)
*Brutal, high-stakes edition with extreme penalties and player eliminations.*
- **Deck Count:** 168 cards
- [x] **Wild Draw 10 (+10)** — Forces next player to draw 10 cards and lose turn.
- [x] **Wild Draw 6 (+6)** — Forces next player to draw 6 cards and lose turn.
- [x] **Skip Everyone** — Skips all other players; current player takes an immediate extra turn.
- [x] **Discard All (Color Dump)** — Discards all cards of the matching color from hand at once.
- [x] **Wild Color Roulette** — Next player picks a color and must draw until they find that color.
- [x] **The 25-Card Mercy Elimination Rule** — Any player with 25+ cards in hand is eliminated (knocked out).
- [x] **7-0 Hand Shifts** — Playing a `7` forces a hand swap with any chosen player; playing a `0` passes all hands in play direction.
- [x] **Draw Card Stacking** — Chain draw penalties (+2, +4, +6, +10) to pass accumulated cards to the next player.

---

### 2. 🌓 UNO Flip! (112 Cards)
*Double-sided deck featuring a mild "Light Side" and a punishing "Dark Side".*
- **Deck Count:** 112 double-sided cards
- [x] **Flip Action Card** — Flips the discard pile, draw deck, and all players' hands to the opposite side.
- [x] **Light Side Cards:**
  - Classic 0–9 Numbers, Draw 1 (+1), Skip, Reverse, Wild, Wild Draw 2.
- [x] **Dark Side Cards:**
  - **Draw 5 (+5)** — High penalty draw.
  - **Dark Skip Everyone** — Skips all players, returning turn to you.
  - **Dark Wild Draw Color** — Next player draws until they get a specific color chosen by the player.
- [x] **Dynamic Card Flip Animation** — Visual 3D flip effect transforming the entire game board between Light and Dark modes.

---

### 3. ⚡ UNO Flex (112 Cards)
*Strategic twist allowing cards to flex into secondary colors and supercharged actions.*
- **Deck Count:** 112 cards
- [x] **Flex Cards (Dual-Color & Dual-Action)** — Each flex card has a primary state and a secondary "flex" state (e.g. Green 4 flexes into Red 4).
- [x] **Flex Action Cards:**
  - **Flex Draw 4** — Player chooses who draws the 4 cards instead of the next player automatically.
  - **Flex Skip** — Skips all other players.
  - **Flex Draw 2** — Forces all other players to draw 1 card each.
- [x] **Power Cards (Green / Red Status)** — Each player has a 2-sided Power Card. Using a flex ability flips the power card to Red (exhausted); playing power-flip number cards recharges it back to Green.

---

### 4. 🚀 UNO Attack / UNO Extreme (112 Cards)
*Mechanical card launcher simulation with randomized card bursts.*
- **Deck Count:** 112 cards
- [x] **Electronic / Mechanical Launcher Simulator** — Pressing the launcher triggers a randomized shoot animation (0 to 8+ cards ejected).
- [x] **Attack! Card** — Forces target player to press the launcher button.
- [x] **Hit 2 Card** — Forces target player to press the launcher button twice.
- [x] **Discard All Card** — Discards all cards of the matching color in one turn.
- [x] **Wild Attack-Attack Card** — Choose color and choose an opponent to press the launcher twice.

---

### 5. 🃏 UNO All Wild (112 Cards)
*Every single card in the deck is a Wild card — no color or number matching required.*
- **Deck Count:** 112 cards (100% Wilds)
- [x] **Wild Skip & Wild Skip Two** — Skip the next 1 or 2 players.
- [x] **Wild Targeted Draw 2** — Pick any player at the table to draw 2 cards without skipping their turn.
- [x] **Wild Forced Swap** — Exchange your entire hand with any chosen opponent.
- [x] **Wild Reverse & Wild Draw 4**.

---

## ⚡ House Rules & Advanced Modifiers

- [x] **Jump-In Rule** — If holding an exact identical card (same color + same symbol/number), play it instantly out of turn.
- [x] **Draw Until Playable Toggle** — Keep drawing cards until you draw a card you can play.
- [x] **Wild +4 Challenge Rule** — Challenge the authenticity of a played +4 to check if the opponent had a matching color.
- [x] **Custom Stacking Chains** — Configurable +2 on +2, +4 on +2, +6, and +10 rules.
